Myths and big questions

Five things that are not true about black holes, and the questions nobody can answer yet.

Myths

✗ Black holes are cosmic vacuum cleaners

Far away, a black hole pulls like any object of the same mass. Only very close does its gravity become extreme.

✗ The Sun will become a black hole

The Sun is too light. It will end as a white dwarf.

✗ A black hole is a hole

It is an object with a great deal of mass, and a region around it, not an empty hole.

✗ One could swallow Earth soon

The nearest known black hole is about 1,600 light-years away. No known black hole is anywhere near us.

✗ Particle colliders will make dangerous ones

Cosmic rays with far more energy than any collider have hit Earth for billions of years, and nothing has happened.

Hawking radiation

In 1974 Stephen Hawking showed that quantum effects make a black hole give off a very faint heat. The bigger the black hole, the colder it is. A black hole with the mass of the Sun would be about 60 billionths of a degree above absolute zero, colder than the 2.7 degrees of space itself, so today it takes in more than it gives out.

Evaporating such a black hole would take far longer than the age of the universe, about 13.8 billion years. Hawking radiation has never been observed. It is a prediction from theory.

Questions still open

What is at the centre?

We need a theory that joins gravity and quantum physics.

Where does the information go?

If a black hole evaporates, what happens to everything that fell in? This is the information paradox.

How did giants grow so fast?

Huge black holes already existed when the universe was under a billion years old.

Are wormholes real?

They appear in the equations, but there is no evidence they exist.
