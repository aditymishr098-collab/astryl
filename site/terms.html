1. About these terms

Astryl (astryl.space) is a free, open-source website about space, made by Adity Mishra. By using the website you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use it. You can read everything without an account.

2. What Astryl is for

Astryl is for learning and for fun. We work hard to keep the numbers, dates and positions right, and we name our sources, but mistakes can happen. Do not use Astryl for navigation, for safety decisions, for school or professional work that needs a guaranteed source, or as the only source for anything important. Never look at the Sun without a proper solar filter, whatever a page or a photo here shows.

3. Your account

  • An account is optional. It needs a username, a name to show and a password; we do not ask for your email address, and the name does not have to be your real one.
  • Your name, username and scores are public: they appear on what you share, on the leaderboards and on your profile.
  • Choose a name and a username that are not rude, do not pretend to be someone else or the Astryl team, and do not contain personal details.
  • Keep your password and your recovery code safe. Without them we cannot give the account back to you, because we have no email address to check who you are.
  • You can change your name at any time, your username once every 30 days, and delete your account at any time on the Account page.
  • If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before making an account.

4. Community and comments

In the Astryl Community and in the comments on the blog, you agree to:

  • be kind: no insults, hate, threats or bullying;
  • share only photos you took yourself, and only of the sky;
  • stay on the subject of space and the night sky;
  • not post links, advertising, spam, or anything illegal or sexual;
  • not share personal details, yours or anyone else’s (phone numbers, addresses, emails);
  • not try to break, overload or misuse the website, its quizzes or its leaderboards.

Anyone can report a photo, post or comment. Reported items are checked by a moderator and may be hidden or removed. We may remove any content, and close accounts that break these rules, without warning.

5. What you share stays yours

Your photos and words remain yours. By posting them you allow Astryl to show them on the website, with your username or the name you typed, for as long as you leave them there. You can delete your own photos, posts and comments at any time, and they are removed when you delete your account (guest blog comments can be deleted from the same browser that posted them, or on request).

6. Astryl’s content and code

The source code of Astryl is public on GitHub and may be used under the licence in that repository. The photos on Astryl come from NASA, ESA, ISRO and other sources named under each photo; they keep their own licences and credits. The name “Astryl” and its logo belong to the project: please do not use them in a way that suggests your project is Astryl.

7. Quizzes, points and leaderboards

Space Points, streaks, badges and leaderboard places are for fun only. They have no money value and cannot be exchanged for anything. Scores gained by cheating or by automated tools may be removed.

8. Links and outside services

10. Your privacy

What we store and why is explained in the Privacy Policy, and in short form on the Your data page.

11. Changes and contact
