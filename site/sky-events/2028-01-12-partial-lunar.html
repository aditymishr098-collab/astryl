Partial lunar eclipse of 12 January 2028

Only part of the Moon enters Earth’s dark shadow, so a dark bite appears on one side of the full Moon.

Key facts

12 January 2028Date
04:13 UTMiddle of the eclipse
0.066Magnitude

When and where it can be seen

A lunar eclipse can be seen from every place where the Moon is above the horizon at the time. The table shows the middle of the eclipse in local time.
