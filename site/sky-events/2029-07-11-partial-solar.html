🌞 Partial solar eclipse of 11 July 2029

The Moon covers only part of the Sun, which looks like a cookie with a bite taken out.

Key facts

11 July 2029Date
15:36 UTMiddle of the eclipse
0.230Magnitude

For your city

Where it can be seen

A small partial eclipse over the far south of South America.

⚠️ Never look at the Sun directly, not even during an eclipse. Use certified eclipse glasses (ISO 12312-2), a welder’s glass no. 14, or a pinhole projector. Sunglasses are not safe.

Date, time and magnitude: NASA eclipse pages (eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov). Times are the middle of the eclipse; partial phases start earlier and end later.
