🗂️ Your data

Last updated 17 November 2026 · a plain summary of the Privacy Policy

Astryl has no ads and sells nothing about you. Without an account almost nothing is stored about you at all. This page shows what is kept when you do use an account or post something, and how to see, change or delete it.

NoAds or trackers
NoEmail address or real name needed
NoSelling or sharing of your data
1 tapTo delete your account and everything with it

What is stored

WhatExactly what is keptWhyHow long
Username and passwordYour username; your password only as a scrambled hash that nobody can read, not even usTo log you inUntil you delete the account
Recovery codeOnly a scrambled hash of the codeSo you can set a new passwordUntil you make a new code or delete the account
Your settingsThe switches you chose (shown on the leaderboard or not, and so on)To do what you askedUntil you change them or delete the account

Blog commentsThe name you typed (or “Guest”), your comment, and scrambled ids used only to limit spamTo show the comment and stop spamUntil you delete it, or it is removed
ReportsWhich item was reported and why, with a scrambled id of the reporterSo a moderator can check itUntil the moderator decides
Visit countsA count per page, day and country. No name, no account, no internet addressTo see which pages are usefulAs totals only

What stays only on your device

Your theme (light or dark), your city for sky times, things you saved before making an account, your “days in a row” count on the home page and a random id used for comments and reports are kept in your browser only. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

What is never stored

  • Your password in readable form.
  • Your internet (IP) address. For rate limits we keep only a scrambled form that changes every day.
  • Your exact location. Sky times are worked out in your browser.
  • The camera and location details inside photos you upload.

What you can do

👀 See it

Your Account page and your public profile show what is tied to your account. My space shows your saved things.

✏️ Change it

Change your username, your leaderboard switch and your password on the Account page. Edit your own photos and posts from the ⋯ menu in the community.

🗑️ Delete it

Delete single photos, posts and comments yourself. “Delete my account” on the Account page removes the account and everything stored with it, straight away.

📬 Ask for a copy

Write to contact@astryl.space with your username to get a copy of your data or to have something corrected.

Where it is kept

Astryl’s data is kept in a Supabase database that only Astryl’s own server code can read; the website itself is served by GitHub Pages. The details, including the old Android app, are in the Privacy Policy. The rules for using Astryl are in the Terms and Conditions.
