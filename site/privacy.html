Privacy policy

Astryl is made by Adity Mishra. It has no ads, no account and no tracking. This page says exactly what the app and this website do with your data.

The Android app

  • Everything works without internet, except the optional online features below. Planet data, positions and 3D surfaces are calculated on your phone.
  • Online features are off by default. If you switch them on in Settings, the app contacts api.wheretheiss.at (or celestrak.org) for orbit data, ll.thespacedevs.com for the launch list, and api.spaceflightnewsapi.net for news. Nothing about you is sent. Those services can only see your phone's internet address.
  • Besides that, the internet is used only for updates. The app asks GitHub for the latest release and, when you tap Download and install, downloads the signed APK from that release. GitHub sees your phone's internet address, like any web request. The daily check can be turned off in Settings.
  • Your birth date, city and settings are saved only on your phone. Android's cloud backup is switched off for Astryl. The backup file in Settings is made only when you ask, and goes only where you send it.

The web version (astryl.space/app)

  • The app runs in your browser. Planet positions, the Moon, the sky and the quiz are calculated in your browser.
  • The web version saves nothing: no cookies and no local storage of your choices. When you close the tab, your city, favourites and quiz scores are gone.
  • It has no online features, so it contacts no space data services.
  • The engine that draws the app is served from this site.
  • Some languages, such as Hindi, may need a font your device does not have. The web engine can then download one from Google's font servers, which would see your internet address. To avoid that, use English or the Android app.

This website

  • It has no analytics, no cookies, no ads and no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical logs such as your internet address when you visit, under its own privacy statement.
