How to find them

Start with Orion (Mriga in Indian astronomy), high in the evening sky from December to March. Red Betelgeuse is its left shoulder, blue Rigel its right foot. Follow the three belt stars down and left to Sirius, the brightest star of the night. Follow them up and right to orange Aldebaran (Rohini).

For Polaris, find the Saptarishi (Big Dipper) and follow its two pointer stars. The Constellations and Tonight’s sky pages show where each star is from your city right now.
