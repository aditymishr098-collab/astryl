When and how it formed

About 4.6 billion years ago, with JupiterFormed
Slowly, perhaps over a million years or moreHow long it took

Callisto formed farthest out among Jupiter's big moons and grew slowly, so it never got hot enough to fully separate rock from ice. It is not squeezed by tides, so its surface is the oldest and most cratered in the solar system.

First million years or more

Rock and ice slowly gather in the outer part of Jupiter's disk.

About 4 billion years ago

Heavy bombardment covers it in craters, including the giant Valhalla basin.

Since then

Almost unchanged; Galileo found signs of a salty ocean deep inside.

All dates are estimates from meteorites, moon rocks, space missions and computer models.

Did you know?

Callisto is one of the most heavily cratered places in the solar system, and its surface is very old.

Callisto is far enough from Jupiter to get less of its harsh radiation, so scientists have suggested it for a future base.
