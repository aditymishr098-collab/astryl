When and how it formed

About 4.6 billion years agoFormed
Probably a few tens of millions of yearsHow long it took

Rocky grains near the hot young Sun stuck together into bigger and bigger bodies that crashed and merged. Mercury ended up with a giant iron core that fills most of the planet.

4.6 billion years ago

Dust and rock near the Sun clump into small planet seeds.

First tens of millions of years

The seeds collide and merge into Mercury; heavy iron sinks to the centre.

About 4.1 to 3.8 billion years ago

Big impacts and lava floods shape the surface; huge basins like Caloris form.

Since then

The planet slowly cools and shrinks, wrinkling its crust into long cliffs.

❓ Still unknown: Why is the iron core so big? A giant impact may have knocked away much of the rocky outer layer, but MESSENGER found easily-lost elements like sulfur and potassium on the surface, which a very violent past would have boiled away.

All dates are estimates from meteorites, moon rocks, space missions and computer models.

Did you know?

One day on Mercury, from sunrise to the next sunrise, lasts about 176 Earth days. That is twice as long as its year.

Mercury has almost no air, so its sky is black even by day, and its nights are freezing cold.
