When and how it formed

About 4.54 billion years agoFormed
About 30 to 100 million yearsHow long it took

Earth grew from rocky planet seeds that collided and merged. Heavy iron sank to form the core. A Mars-sized body called Theia then hit the young Earth, and the debris formed the Moon.

4.54 billion years ago

Planet seeds merge; Earth grows and heats up.

In the first ~30 million years

Iron sinks to the centre and forms the core.

About 4.5 billion years ago

The giant impact with Theia; the Moon forms from the debris.

About 4.4 billion years ago

The oldest known crystals (zircons) show that liquid water already existed.

At least 3.5 billion years ago

Life appears; about 2.4 billion years ago microbes fill the air with oxygen.

All dates are estimates from meteorites, moon rocks, space missions and computer models.
