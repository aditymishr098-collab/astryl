When and how it formed

About 4.6 billion years ago, with SaturnFormed
Within the first few million yearsHow long it took

Titan formed from rock and ice in the disk around Saturn. It is the only moon with a thick atmosphere, mostly nitrogen, which may have come from ammonia ice broken apart long ago. Today it has rain, rivers and lakes of liquid methane.

First few million years

Rock and ice gather into a moon bigger than Mercury.

Early on (unsure)

Its thick nitrogen air forms, perhaps from ammonia in its ice.

Today

Methane rain fills lakes near the poles; an ocean of water lies deep below the ice.

❓ Still unknown: Where Titan's methane comes from, since sunlight keeps destroying it, is still a puzzle. NASA's Dragonfly drone is due to arrive in 2034.

All dates are estimates from meteorites, moon rocks, space missions and computer models.

Did you know?

Titan is the only moon with a thick atmosphere. Its air pressure at the surface is higher than on Earth.

Titan has rivers and lakes, but they hold liquid methane and ethane, not water.
