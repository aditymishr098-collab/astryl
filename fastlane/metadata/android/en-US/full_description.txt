Astryl is an offline-first solar system app. Planet positions, the Moon, sunrise and sunset, eclipses and the star map are all calculated on your phone.

<b>Explore</b>
• 2D map with the real position of every planet on any date from 1800 to 2050
• Rotating 3D worlds: the Sun, 8 planets, Pluto, our Moon and 7 famous moons
• Facts, atmosphere and oxygen, "could humans live here?", compare two worlds
• Your weight, jump height and age on other planets, and a quiz

<b>Tonight's sky</b>
• Which planets you can see tonight from your city, no location permission needed
• Moon phase, moonrise and moonset, a Moon calendar and the sky on any date
• Sun and light: first light, golden hours, sunset and the dark-sky window
• Star map with about 1,000 stars and 89 constellations, drag and pinch to explore
• Binocular guide with 12 sights and where to look tonight
• Eclipse calendar and daily reminders
• Share a picture of tonight's sky

<b>Optional online features (off by default)</b>
• Where the International Space Station, Tiangong and Hubble are, and when they pass over your city
• Upcoming rocket launches and space news
Nothing about you is sent. Switch the setting off and the app never goes online.

<b>Private</b>
No ads, no account, no tracking. Works in English and Hindi. Open source, AGPL-3.0 licence.
